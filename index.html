What we build
Thurin.id

Your PGP key, bound to your Ethereum address. Look up any name and see the claim and the proofs behind it, or prove your own in a few minutes, from the page or a terminal. Anyone can check it, and none of it depends on us.

What we believe

No one can switch it off.

Identities live on Ethereum, a public record no company controls. There is no account to suspend.

Everything is open.

Every line of code is public. Anyone can read it, run it, or fork it.

Private by default.

Nothing about you goes public unless you choose it. No email, no tracking, no analytics.

Nothing taken on faith.

You don’t have to trust us. Anyone can check any claim for themselves, and there is no switch we could flip to change the answer.

No token. No fees. The contract has no owner. How we check ourselves →
