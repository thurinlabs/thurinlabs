No data collection. Just cryptography, smart contracts, and you. Zero-knowledge proofs, on-chain identity claims, and public-key cryptography are the foundation. Signet

Create a permanent, verifiable link between your Ethereum address and your PGP key. Signet walks you through a four-step flow that publishes your cryptographic identity claim on-chain.

  • Many-to-many ETH address and PGP key mapping
  • Dual-signed identity claim: wallet signature + PGP clearsign
  • On-chain registry with O(1) duplicate detection
  • Fully non-custodial, no intermediaries
