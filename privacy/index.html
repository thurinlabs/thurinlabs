Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 2026  |  Last Updated: January 21, 2026

Overview

Thurin Labs LLC ("Thurin," "we," "our," or "us") provides privacy-preserving identity verification using zero-knowledge cryptography. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Thurin is designed to verify identity claims (such as age or uniqueness) without collecting personal information. Your identity data never leaves your device.

What We Collect

Data We Store

Data Purpose Retention
Ethereum address On-chain verification record Permanent (public blockchain)
Cryptographic nullifier Sybil resistance (prevents duplicate claims) Permanent (public blockchain)
Proof timestamp Verification timing Permanent (public blockchain)

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Name, address, date of birth
  • Driver's license or ID numbers
  • Photos, biometrics, or facial data
  • Social Security numbers
  • Any raw data from your mobile driver's license

How Verification Works

  1. You present your mobile driver's license from Apple or Google Wallet
  2. Your device generates a zero-knowledge proof locally
  3. The proof cryptographically confirms claims (e.g., "valid license" or "unique person") without revealing underlying data
  4. Only the proof result is transmitted—your personal data stays on your device

Third Parties

We do not share data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

Service providers:

  • Blockchain networks (Ethereum, Base, etc.) store verification records on-chain
  • No third-party analytics or tracking services

Cookies and Tracking

Our website does not use cookies or tracking technologies.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Know what data we have about you (limited to on-chain records)
  • Request information about our data practices
  • Contact us with privacy concerns

Data Security

  • All verification happens client-side using cryptographic proofs
  • No personal data is transmitted to or stored on our servers
  • On-chain data is public but contains no personally identifiable information

Children's Privacy

Thurin requires a valid mobile driver's license. Users must meet the age requirements of their state's licensing authority.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact Us

For privacy questions or concerns:

  • Email: privacy@thurin.id
  • Website: https://thurin.id

Legal Basis (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area, our legal basis for processing is:

  • Legitimate interest in providing privacy-preserving identity verification
  • No personal data is processed—zero-knowledge proofs ensure data minimization by design

Thurin is committed to building identity verification that respects user privacy. By using zero-knowledge cryptography, we prove what matters without exposing what doesn't.
